## Task

Turn declaration lifecycle events into notifications a declarant can read, on every channel they
subscribed to. It owns the projection and the channel adapters; it owns no declaration state and
makes no decision.

## Why it was extracted

The projection lived inside
[Example Component A](/systems/example-system-a/components/example-component-a/) while there were
two channels. A third channel — the trade portal inbox — made the case: notification changes were
forcing deployments of the component that owns the declaration lifecycle, and a bug in a channel
adapter could take intake down with it.

The system documentation had recorded this as a reversible decision, with the trigger written
down; see
[Decomposition Rationale](/systems/example-system-a/system-architecture/building-block-view/decomposition-rationale/)
and debt item **D-1** in
[11. Risks and Technical Debt](/systems/example-system-a/system-architecture/risks-and-technical-debt/).

## Quality goals

| Priority | Goal | Scenario |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Intake stays independent** | A failing channel adapter never affects declaration intake. |
| 2 | **At-most-one notification per event and channel** | A redelivered event does not notify the declarant twice. |
| 3 | **Deployable without intake** | A channel change ships without redeploying Example Component A. |
