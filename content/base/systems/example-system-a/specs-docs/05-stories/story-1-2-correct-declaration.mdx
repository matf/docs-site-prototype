> **As a** declarant
> **I want** to correct an accepted declaration before it is cleared
> **so that** a re-weighing at the warehouse does not force me to start again.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** an accepted declaration, **when** a correction is submitted, **then** a new revision is
   stored and the previous risk decision is invalidated.
2. **Given** a cleared declaration, **when** a correction is submitted, **then** it is refused with
   a reason.
3. **Given** a correction written against a stale revision, **when** it is submitted, **then** it is
   refused rather than silently applied.
4. **Given** a risk decision arrives for a superseded revision, **when** it is processed, **then**
   it is discarded without changing the declaration state.

## What this story taught us

Criterion 4 was not in the original story. It surfaced during implementation as a race between the
correction and an in-flight assessment, and is now documented as a runtime scenario in its own
right: [Correction Race Scenario](/systems/example-system-a/components/example-component-a/system-architecture/runtime-view/correction-race/).
