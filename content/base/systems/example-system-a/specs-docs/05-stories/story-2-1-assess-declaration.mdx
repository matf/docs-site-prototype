> **As a** customs officer
> **I want** every accepted declaration to carry a risk decision with its reasons
> **so that** I can act on a hold without asking anybody what caused it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** an accepted declaration revision, **when** it is assessed, **then** exactly one
   decision is produced, carrying the band, the rules that fired and the rule set and model
   versions.
2. **Given** the scoring model is unavailable, **when** a declaration is assessed, **then** a
   conservative fallback decision is produced and marked as such — never silence.
3. **Given** the same inputs and versions, **when** the case is re-evaluated years later, **then**
   the band is identical.

## Open

Criterion 3 currently assumes the evaluator code is unchanged; the evaluator version is not yet
part of the decision. That gap is tracked as debt item CB-D1 in
[Example Component B — 11. Risks and Technical Debt](/systems/example-system-a/system-architecture/building-block-view/example-component-b/component-architecture/risks-and-technical-debt/)
and is why this story is still in review.
