## Goal

A declarant can submit a declaration and knows within seconds whether it was accepted, and if not,
exactly what to change.

## Why this is one epic

Submission, rejection feedback and correction are one user journey. Splitting them would have
delivered a system that accepts declarations but cannot tell anybody what is wrong with them —
which is worse than not shipping.

## Acceptance

- A valid declaration is acknowledged and durably stored.
- An invalid declaration is rejected with a rule identifier, a field path and the accepted values.
- A correction is accepted until the declaration is cleared, and refused afterwards.
- No declaration acknowledged to the portal is ever lost.

The last point is the one that shaped the architecture — it is why
[ADR-0001 of Component A](/systems/example-system-a/components/example-component-a/system-architecture/architecture-decisions/adr-0001-transactional-outbox/)
exists.
