## Goal

Every accepted declaration gets a risk decision that a customs officer can act on and an auditor
can reproduce, and analysts can change the rules behind it without waiting for a release.

## Why the analyst requirements are in this epic

FR-6 and FR-7 look like operational tooling rather than product scope. They are here because they
are what makes the risk decision *useful*: a rule set that takes a release cycle to change is a
rule set that is always out of date. Separating them would have made them somebody else's backlog.

## Acceptance

- Every accepted declaration revision produces exactly one decision.
- A decision names the rules that fired and the versions that produced it.
- A high-band decision produces an inspection order.
- A new rule set takes effect within five minutes with no deployment, and rolls back in one step.
