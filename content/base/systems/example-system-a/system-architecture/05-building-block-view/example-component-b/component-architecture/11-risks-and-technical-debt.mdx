## Risks

| # | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CB-R1 | A rule set passes simulation but behaves differently in production. | High — mass holds or mass clearances | Medium | 60-minute observation window and one-step rollback — [Rule Set Rollout](/systems/example-system-a/system-architecture/building-block-view/example-component-b/component-architecture/runtime-view/rule-set-rollout/). |
| CB-R2 | Prolonged fallback operation goes unnoticed. | Medium — decisions are conservative but not model-based | Low | `risk_fallback_ratio` on the *Risk decisions* dashboard; warning above 5 %. |
| CB-R3 | History features drift as the upstream store changes its aggregation. | Medium — silent score drift | Medium | Feature schema versioned and asserted; drift monitor on the feature distributions. |
| CB-R4 | The evaluator code changes behaviour, breaking reproducibility of old decisions. | High — audit exposure | Low | Debt item D-2 at system level: the evaluator version is not yet stamped into the decision. |

## Technical debt

| # | Debt | Why | Cost | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CB-D1 | The evaluator version is not part of the decision event. | Not foreseen at launch — system debt item D-2. | Reproducibility assumes the evaluator is unchanged. | Add `evaluatorVersion` to `RiskAssessmentCompleted` 1.5.0. |
| CB-D2 | Shadow scoring runs in the same workload as production scoring. | Simpler deployment. | A slow shadow model adds latency to real decisions. | Separate workload once a second model family is introduced. |
| CB-D3 | Simulation uses 30 days of cases regardless of seasonality. | The window was chosen for simulation runtime. | A rule set validated in July may behave differently in December. | Seasonal window selection; specialist unit is aware. |
