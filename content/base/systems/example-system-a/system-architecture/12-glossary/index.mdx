## Domain terms

Agreed with the specialist unit. Where the everyday word and the system's word differ, the
difference is stated — that is the whole point of this table.

| Term | Meaning in this system |
| --- | --- |
| **Declaration** | The formal statement about a consignment crossing the border. The unit of work of the whole system. |
| **Revision** | An immutable version of a declaration. A correction creates a new revision; nothing is ever overwritten. |
| **Consignment** | The goods covered by one declaration, moving from one consignor to one consignee. |
| **Declarant** | The party legally responsible for the declaration. Not necessarily the party that submitted it. |
| **Clearance** | The decision that a consignment may proceed. Terminal state of the happy path. |
| **Hold** | The decision that a consignment must not proceed until a control decision exists. |
| **Risk case** | The assessment record for one declaration revision. Owned by the risk engine, not by the declaration. |
| **Score band** | `low`, `elevated` or `high`. The band, not the raw score, drives the decision. |
| **Rule set** | A versioned collection of customs and risk rules, deployed as data. |

## Terms deliberately not used

| Avoided | Use instead | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Customer" | Declarant | The declarant has a legal role, not a commercial one. |
| "Order" | Declaration | "Order" means an inspection order in this domain. |
| "Approved" | Cleared | Approval suggests discretion; clearance is a rule-based outcome. |
