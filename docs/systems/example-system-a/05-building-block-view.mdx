## Level 1 — decomposition into components

Example System A is decomposed along the lifecycle of a declaration: one component owns the
declaration and its state, another owns the risk decision. The split follows the ownership of the
data, not the technical layers, which is why neither component reads the other's database.

## Why the decomposition stops here

A third component for notification was considered and rejected: notifications are a thin
projection of declaration events, and a separate deployable would have added an operational unit
without adding an ownership boundary. The projection lives inside Example Component A. This is
tracked as a reversible decision in
[11. Risks and Technical Debt](/systems/example-system-a/risks-and-technical-debt/).
