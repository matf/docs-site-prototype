## Task

Example System A accepts electronic goods declarations for consignments crossing the Swiss
border, checks them against the applicable customs rules, determines the control risk and
releases or withholds the consignment. It replaces a batch-oriented predecessor and is expected
to give declarants a decision within seconds instead of hours.

The system is used by three groups: **declarants and freight forwarders** submitting
declarations through the trade portal, **customs officers** who act on withheld consignments,
and **risk analysts** who maintain the rule sets and scoring models.

## Quality goals

The three goals below drive the architecture. Everything else is negotiable.

| Priority | Quality goal | Motivation | Scenario |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Traceability** | Every clearance decision must be reconstructable years later for legal review. | Given a declaration reference, an auditor can reproduce the decision, the rule set version and the model version that produced it. |
| 2 | **Availability during opening hours** | A stalled clearance blocks physical traffic at the border. | 99.8 % availability 05:00–23:00 CET; no single component failure stops declaration intake. |
| 3 | **Throughput at peak** | Traffic is strongly seasonal and concentrated in the morning. | 4 000 declarations per hour sustained, 250 per minute in bursts, 95th percentile decision latency below 8 seconds. |

Confidentiality and data protection are treated as constraints rather than goals — they are
non-negotiable and documented under
[Security and Data Protection](/systems/example-system-a/crosscutting-concerns/security-and-data-protection/).

## Stakeholders

| Role | Expectation |
| --- | --- |
| Declarant / freight forwarder | Immediate, comprehensible feedback on rejected declarations. |
| Customs officer | Reliable, well-explained risk decisions with the evidence attached. |
| Risk analyst | Rule sets and scoring models can be changed without a software release. |
| Operations | Observable system with actionable alerts and a documented recovery procedure. |
| Legal and data protection | Retention, access and erasure rules provably enforced. |
