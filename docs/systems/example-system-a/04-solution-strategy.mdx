## The approach in five decisions

| Approach | Addresses | Recorded in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Event-driven decomposition.** Components communicate through durable business events on the central broker; no synchronous chains between components. | Availability, traceability | [ADR-0001](/systems/example-system-a/architecture-decisions/adr-0001-event-driven-integration/) |
| **The event log is the audit record.** Decision events are immutable, versioned and retained; audit is a consumer, not a separate write path. | Traceability | [ADR-0001](/systems/example-system-a/architecture-decisions/adr-0001-event-driven-integration/) |
| **Rules and models as versioned data.** Rule sets and scoring models are deployed independently of the software and referenced by version in every decision. | Changeability, traceability | [Example Component B — 4. Solution Strategy](/systems/example-system-a/components/example-component-b/solution-strategy/) |
| **Hexagonal components.** Each component isolates domain logic behind adapters, so the broker, the database and the external APIs stay replaceable. | Testability, changeability | [Development Guidelines](/systems/example-system-a/crosscutting-concerns/development-guidelines/) |
| **Swiss-hosted, two data centres, active/passive.** | Data residency, availability | [ADR-0002](/systems/example-system-a/architecture-decisions/adr-0002-hosting-and-data-residency/) |

## Why event-driven rather than a request chain

The obvious alternative — the intake component calling risk assessment synchronously and waiting
for the answer — was rejected for two reasons that both come from
[1. Introduction and Goals](/systems/example-system-a/introduction-and-goals/):

1. **Availability.** A synchronous chain makes intake unavailable whenever risk assessment is
   unavailable. With events, declarations continue to be accepted and are assessed as soon as the
   engine recovers; only the decision is delayed.
2. **Traceability.** A synchronous call leaves no durable record of the request. The event log
   gives the ten-year reconstruction requirement almost for free.

The price is paid in complexity, and it is a real price: eventual consistency in the declaration
state, duplicate delivery to handle, and a replay procedure operations has to know. Those costs
are documented rather than hidden — see
[11. Risks and Technical Debt](/systems/example-system-a/risks-and-technical-debt/) and
[Operations and Monitoring](/systems/example-system-a/crosscutting-concerns/operations-and-monitoring/).

## Quality goal coverage

| Quality goal | How the strategy delivers it |
| --- | --- |
| Traceability | Immutable decision events, rule set and model version stamped into every decision. |
| Availability during opening hours | No synchronous inter-component dependency; two data centres; intake degrades to the tariff cache. |
| Throughput at peak | Independently scalable consumers; the broker absorbs bursts instead of the database. |
