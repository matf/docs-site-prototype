## Authentication

| Caller | Mechanism | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Declarant via Trade Portal | OIDC at the portal; the portal signs the message to the broker | The system never sees the declarant's credentials. |
| Risk analyst | OIDC, federal identity provider, second factor required | Role `risk-analyst`. |
| Customs officer | OIDC, second factor required | Read access to declarations, no write. |
| Service-to-service | mTLS on the broker with per-component certificates | Rotated every 90 days by the platform. |

There is no local user store and no local password handling — constraint **T-4**.

## Authorisation

Authorisation is enforced in the component that owns the data, never in a gateway alone.

| Role | May | May not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `declarant` | Submit and correct own declarations; read own declarations | See other declarants' data, see risk scores |
| `customs-officer` | Read any declaration and the reasons for a hold | Change a declaration, change a risk decision |
| `risk-analyst` | Publish rule sets and models, trigger recalculation | Read declarant identity beyond the risk features |
| `operator` | Read technical state, replay dead-lettered messages | Read declaration payloads |

The last row is deliberate: an operator can recover the system without reading business data.
Message payloads in operational tooling are shown as hashes, and the replay procedure works on
message identifiers.

## Data classification and retention

| Data | Classification | Retention | Erasure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Declaration payload | Confidential — commercially sensitive | 10 years | Legal hold; no early erasure |
| Declarant identity | Personal data | 10 years | Pseudonymised after 10 years, not deleted (legal obligation) |
| Risk score and rule hits | Confidential — internal | 10 years | Never exposed to the declarant |
| Technical logs | Internal | 90 days | Automatic, no payloads in logs |
| Dead-letter messages | Confidential | 30 days | Encrypted at rest; access logged |

Payloads are never written to application logs. This is enforced by a serialiser that refuses to
log annotated fields — described in
[Development Guidelines](/systems/example-system-a/crosscutting-concerns/development-guidelines/)
and verified by an architecture test in each component's build.

## Threats explicitly considered

| Threat | Mitigation | Residual risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forged declaration submitted in another declarant's name | Portal-side authentication plus message signing; the intake component verifies the signature | Compromised portal account — accepted, monitored by the portal |
| Risk rules disclosed to a declarant | Risk scores and rule identifiers never leave the internal boundary; rejection reasons are customs rules only | Inference over many submissions — tracked in [11. Risks and Technical Debt](/systems/example-system-a/risks-and-technical-debt/) |
| Operator reads business data during an incident | Payload-free operational tooling, access logging | Database-level access by the platform team — covered by the platform's own controls |
