# 1. Introduction and Goals

<DocHeader system="example-system-b" section="introduction-and-goals" status="custom"

## Task

Example System B tells travellers and hauliers what to expect at the border: current waiting
times, opening hours, lane closures and unplanned disruptions. It is a publishing system — it
owns no border process and makes no decision about anybody.

## Quality goals

| Priority | Goal | Scenario |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Correctness of published information** | Nothing is published that is not backed by a current source measurement; stale data is marked stale rather than shown as current. |
| 2 | **Availability under load spikes** | A closure announcement causes a traffic spike of 20× within two minutes; the public site stays up. |
| 3 | **Reach** | An operational notification reaches every opted-in channel within two minutes. |

Goal 1 above goal 2 is deliberate: showing an out-of-date waiting time is worse than showing a
maintenance page.

## Stakeholders

| Role | Expectation |
| --- | --- |
| Traveller / haulier | Accurate, current, plainly worded information |
| Border post operations | Announcements reach the public quickly and can be corrected |
| Communications | Consistent wording across channels |
