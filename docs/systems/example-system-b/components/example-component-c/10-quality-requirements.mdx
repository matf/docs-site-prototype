# 10. Quality Requirements

<DocHeader system="example-system-b" component="example-component-c" section="quality-requirements" status="custom"
  custom={{owner: 'Team Beta — Channels Squad (fictional)', lastReviewed: '2026-07-09'}} />

| # | Quality | Stimulus | Response | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CC-1 | Reach | A broadcast to 40 000 subscribers. | All are attempted. | 95 % delivered within 2 minutes. |
| CC-2 | No duplicates | The same request is delivered twice by the broker. | No subscriber receives a second message. | Unique constraint on (notification, subscriber, channel); verified in an integration test. |
| CC-3 | Honesty | The provider accepts but later bounces. | The delivery record is corrected and `NotificationFailed` is raised. | Bounce webhook processed within 5 minutes. |
| CC-4 | Rate compliance | Fan-out at full speed. | The provider never throttles the sender. | Zero throttling responses in production over the last two quarters. |
