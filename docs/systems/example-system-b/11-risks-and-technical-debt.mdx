# 11. Risks and Technical Debt

<DocHeader system="example-system-b" section="risks-and-technical-debt" status="custom"
  custom={{owner: 'Team Beta — Public Information Services (fictional)', lastReviewed: '2026-07-11'}} />

## Risks

| # | Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BR-1 | A wrong announcement reaches every channel within two minutes and cannot be recalled. | High — public trust | Four-eyes approval before a broadcast; a correction template that references the original. |
| BR-2 | The external e-mail provider throttles a large fan-out. | Medium — delayed reach | Rate-aware batching in [Example Component C](/systems/example-system-b/components/example-component-c/); failures alert operations. |
| BR-3 | Cache invalidation misses a page after a status change. | Medium — stale public information | Invalidation is verified by a probe that reads the public URL after publishing. |

## Technical debt

| # | Debt | Cost | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BD-1 | The Transit Status Service and the Static Publisher are not documented as components in this prototype. | Their arc42 documentation does not exist yet. | Add them as component folders — no navigation change required. |
| BD-2 | Subscription language cannot be changed without re-subscribing. | Support requests. | Planned. |
